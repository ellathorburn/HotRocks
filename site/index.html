What it does

Built for people who alternate between the sauna and the plunge and want a record of it.

Session timer

Start a visit, switch between heat, cold and rest as you go. Rounds can come in any order.

Timeline history

Every visit is saved as an ordered timeline, with heat, cold and rest totals and the temperatures you logged.

Personal records

Longest session, coldest plunge and most rounds in a single visit, kept up to date automatically.

Venues

Tag the sauna or bathhouse you visited so you can look back at where each session happened.

Works offline

Sessions save to your phone first, so a changing room with no signal is fine. They sync to your account when you're back online.

Your data stays yours

Sign in with Apple, Google or an email code. Only you can see your sessions, and you can delete your account and its data from the app.
