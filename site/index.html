HotRocks GitHub

Log your sauna and cold plunge, minute by minute.

HotRocks times each part of your visit: every sauna, every cold plunge and every break, in the order you did them. Coming to iPhone and Android.

Everything from one visit, in one place

Start the timer when you walk in. Tap sauna, cold plunge or break as you move between them, in any order.

See how the visit went

Each session is saved as a timeline, with your time in the sauna, in the plunge and on breaks, plus any temperatures you noted.

Keep your records

Your longest session, coldest plunge and most rounds in one visit, updated every time you log.

Remember where you went

Add the sauna or bathhouse to a session so you can look back at where each one happened.

No signal needed

Sessions save to your phone first, so a changing room with no reception is fine. They back up to your account once you're online again.

Fix it afterwards

Forgot to stop the timer or tapped the wrong thing? Edit any session once you're dressed.

Only yours

Sign in with Apple, Google or an email code. Nobody else can see your sessions, and you can delete your account and everything in it from settings.
